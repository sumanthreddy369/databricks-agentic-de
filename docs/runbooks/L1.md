# L1: Device clock skew

Problem ID: L1. Layer L: Timestamps and matching events in time.

## What happens

A device clock is minutes or hours off, or resets to 1970 after a battery swap.

## Who it affects

Data engineers.

## How to detect it

event_ts in the future or far in the past relative to arrival time.

## What to do

Flag the device; route impossible timestamps to review rather than into Gold windows.

## Autonomy

Level L1 (L1: recommend and escalate to a person; the agent changes nothing).

## Status in this project

Not built yet - handle by hand.
