# M2: Patient identity mismatch

Problem ID: M2. Layer M: Data content problems.

## What happens

Same person under two patient_ids, or one patient_id carrying two people (name or birth date changes between admissions); MRN merges and splits. Counts and SCD1 demographics go wrong.

## Who it affects

Data engineers and data analysts.

## How to detect it

silver_patient_identity_conflicts (one_identity_per_patient_id), silver_patient_duplicate_candidates (one_patient_id_per_person) silver_patient_fuzzy_duplicate_candidates (no_near_duplicate_registration) and silver_patient_name_change_candidates (no_name_change_registration) expectations; MRN merge events.

## What to do

Report the conflicting patient_ids (counts and hashed keys, never names) to data stewards, who decide in the review queue (agent/identity_review.py); never merge or split records automatically.

## Autonomy

Level L1 (L1: recommend and escalate to a person; the agent changes nothing).

## Status in this project

A working tool or check exists in this project. Four Silver checks, scored on Databricks Free Edition against injected ground truth: two people on one id 14/14; one person under two ids 27/27 same details, 18/18 name typo or swapped day/month, 19/19 nickname or new surname; 1 pair raised that is two different people sharing a name and birth date. A steward decides each candidate in the review queue; confirmed duplicates share a person_key in gold.dim_patient_person (1,411 ids -> 1,347 people, matching ground truth).
